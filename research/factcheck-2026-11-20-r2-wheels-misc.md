## Fix list

1. **Lexus ES 2026 Luxury+ (`es-350e-luxury-plus`, `es-500e-luxury-plus-awd`): 21 inch `is_standard: true` is unsupported.**
   - The notes cite a "Lexus Canada pricing release" that is not a listed source.
   - The cited US pressroom page says 21-inch is an optional Wheel & Tire Package, with no grade named.
   - Fix: add the Canadian page as a source and cite it, or set 21 to `is_standard: false`. Either way set `low_confidence: true`, because the grade mapping is not on the cited page.
   - The merged data for `es-350e-luxury-plus` already lists wheels [21], so the 19 and 21 here also differ from what is there. Reconcile.
2. **Volvo EX90 2025 twin-motor, EX40 2025 single-motor, EX30 2025 twin-motor-performance: set `low_confidence: true`.**
   - Each variant's single trim covers several maker trims (Plus and Ultra for EX90 and EX30; Core, Plus and Ultra for EX40).
   - The Cars.com page shows one trim only (EX90 Plus 7-seater, EX40 Core, EX30 AWD Plus).
   - Under the spanning-trim rule, the other trims' sizes could be standard too. Name the trim in the notes. EX40 twin-motor is already `low_confidence`.
3. **Jaguar I-PACE 2020 `ev400`: set `low_confidence: true`.** Cars.com lists S, SE and HSE AWD and gives a wheel size for S only (18 inch). The variant covers all three, so a larger size may also be standard on SE or HSE. Unproven.
4. **Jeep Wagoneer S 2025 `awd` and `awd-2`: set `low_confidence: true`.** The page lists Limited and Launch Edition. Only the Limited's wheel (20 x 8.5) is shown, and the notes already say "Limited AWD". The variant covers both trims. Add "Launch Edition not shown on the page".
5. **Nissan Ariya Platinum+ 20-inch entries.**
   - Duplicates: 2023 `platinum-plus-e-4orce` has two `20 optional` entries (one `low_confidence` from the Cars.com article, one not from the 2023 page). The same applies to 2024 and 2025 `evolve-plus-e-4orce-with-platinum-plus-package`. Keep one entry per size.
   - The Cars.com specs pages give the 20-inch size only in the trim name ("w/20" Wheels") with no spec row. Set `low_confidence: true` on those.
   - The 2024 and 2025 variants carry Canadian names (Evolve+ ... with Platinum+ Package), but the Cars.com pages are US. Say so in the notes.
6. **Toyota C-HR 2026 `awd`:** the Canadian trim is just "AWD", and the 18 standard rests on the US SE page. If the Canadian AWD includes the XSE (20 inch gunmetal), 20 is standard there. Set 18 to `low_confidence: true` and name the market.
7. **Cars.com carry-overs not marked `low_confidence`:**
   - i-MiEV 2012: the variant covers ES and SE, and only ES is shown. Add the trim to the notes.
   - VF 8 Eco 2023 and 2025: the page lists Eco AWD 87.7 kWh. If the variant is the 82 kWh pack, add a note.
8. **Charger Daytona 2024/25 `r-t-awd-2`, Wagoneer S `awd-2`, `r-t-awd` pairs:** identical entries on two variants. Fine if both exist in the data, but confirm the `-2` variants are real separate ratings, so the notes can say which.
