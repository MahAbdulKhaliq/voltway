## Risk points

1. **Taycan 2020-24 heat pump.** `standard` is not supported. Recurrent does not say standard. Porsche Stories (2022) says "optional heat pump in the Taycan" and the batch also cites an InsideEVs 2019 report that says optional. The forum claim of "standard on North American builds" could not be opened (blocked). No NA Porsche document was found. A heat pump that Porsche's own 2022 text calls optional cannot be recorded as `standard`. Recommend `optional` (kind manufacturer, source Porsche Stories, notes: "Porsche Stories 2022 calls it optional; Recurrent shows it on all US trims, which suggests it may have been fitted on many cars; NA standard status unconfirmed"), low_confidence. Alternatively keep Recurrent as a second note, but not as the value's source.
2. **Cayenne.** Heat pump `standard` rests on one non-Porsche page: keep it as community and low_confidence, but flag it as weakly supported (accepted only because the rules allow community; the batch's note about an EV Database snippet is not verifiable). Non-S adapter `ccs1_to_nacs`: reasonable estimate, since the S page says "like all Cayenne Electric models" for the port and "also" for the adapter. Keep `estimated` with low_confidence, and the notes must say that the basis is "S release only, other trims not stated". It is not a manufacturer statement for the non-S trims (the batch correctly says estimated).
3. **Macan 2027 port and adapter.** Porsche USA's 2027 Macan page lists a "Porsche CCS DC Adapter" accessory and never mentions NACS (no NACS or J3400 text on the page). A web search found no Porsche announcement of a NACS port on the 2027 Macan (only a forum thread guessing not before 2028), while the Taycan 2027 is confirmed NACS. So the evidence does not show a NACS Macan, and the CCS1 port in the work list is more likely right. The page's CCS adapter accessory is probably template text shared with the Taycan page. Report to owner: port unresolved, not changed. Adapter value should not be `ccs1_to_nacs_sold` (that name needs a NACS port, which contradicts the data). With the port as `ccs1`, use `nacs_to_ccs1` carried from the 2026 in-box offer (same adapter, no source for 2027), kind manufacturer, low_confidence, notes "Carried from MY2026 'will include' policy; Porsche USA 2027 page lists a CCS DC adapter accessory, port not confirmed as CCS1 vs NACS". If the owner confirms a NACS port, `ccs1_to_nacs_sold` is right.
4. **2026 "in the box".** Both newsrooms say "MY2026 Taycan and Macan Electric will include" the adapter, with no cut-off date or exception. Supported for all 2026 deliveries after the announcement. The batch's note that cars delivered before it may have got the free offer instead is a sensible caveat. Keep `nacs_to_ccs1` low_confidence (already set).
5. **2025 Taycan / 2024-25 Macan `_on_request`.** Supported in both markets: MY2025 Taycan and all existing Macan Electric customers are "eligible for a complimentary adapter". The notice and reservation arrive via My Porsche, with no deadline stated. The batch notes say the same. The brief wants the claim window in the notes: it is "none stated" and that is recorded. OK. Note: Macan 2024-25 is `on_request` but a new Macan 2025 sold after Sept 2025 may have shipped with the adapter (unknown); the notes cover the "existing owners" case only.
