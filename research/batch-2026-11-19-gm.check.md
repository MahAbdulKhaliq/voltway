## Answers to the flagged points

**Silverado pack table (Standard / Extended / Max)**
- **DC 220 / 300 / 350 kW and voltage: verified on the 2026 PDF.** Quotes: "475V up to 220kW on 4WT (14MOD). 670V up to 300kW on 5WT (20MOD). 800V up to 350kW on 5WT (24MOD)" (the last "5WT" is GM's typo for 8WT). The 2025 PDF shows "670 volt, up to 300 kW" and "800 volt, up to 350 kW" too. The Weekly Driver 20-module page says "300 kW" and its 24-module page says "350 kW". **The batch's Extended 300 kW is right; existing records with 350 kW on Extended are wrong** (see Required changes 8).
- **Voltage class.**
  - 800v for Max is fine (GM: "800 volt").
  - 475 V for Standard is GM's charge voltage, not a nominal voltage. Weekly Driver gives 470 V nominal, so 400v is reasonable, but it is a judgement. Label it `estimated`: the batch has `manufacturer` on 4 Standard powertrains.
  - 800v for Extended rests on a 670 V charge voltage. Weekly Driver lists 290 V nominal for the 20-module pack and InsideEVs gives 345.6 V nominal for the 24-module pack with split-pack 800 V charging. So the class depends on whether "class" means charge or nominal voltage. The batch's reasoning is thin. It matches the existing records, so I'd keep it, flagged for the owner.
- **Usable 119 / 170 / 204.**
  - 204 is verified: InsideEVs gives "204 kWh usable (reported)" for the 24-module RST pack. Weekly Driver lists the same pack as 205 kWh "published", unspecified.
  - 119 and 170 are 204 x modules/24. The rules say "never estimate from a different pack size", so this basis is weak. The module counts are from the GM guide. Two readable pages state the figures themselves, and the batch should cite them: Weekly Driver `gm-ultium-truck-14` says 119 kWh and `gm-ultium-truck-20` says 170 kWh, both "as published" (GM doesn't say gross or usable). Green Cars Compare (seen only in search snippets) lists 119 and 173 usable.
  - 119 matches an EPA "pack energy" figure, so it may be gross. Keep both as `estimated`, low confidence, and say gross-or-usable is unconfirmed.
- **10-80 % column mapping (40 / 42 / 46 min, 74 / 111 / 124 mi).**
  - The text stream pairs each time with its mileage: "46 minutes" next to "124 miles", "42 minutes" next to "111 miles", and "40 minutes" with "74 miles". Mileage rises with pack size, so the mapping 4WT = 40, 5WT = 42, 8WT = 46 is sound. The 2025 PDF shows "46 minutes" and "124 miles" next to 8WT.
  - The row label is "Low-80% time to charge", not "10-80 %". It may start from a low SoC other than 10 %. The batch's own notes say "Low-80%", but the field is `published_10_80_min`. I would keep it as `manufacturer` for the 2026 WT, with low confidence, and note the label.
- **2024 3WT / 4WT mapping: supported.** Weekly Driver lists the 20-module pack (170 kWh, 300 kW) for "Silverado EV (2024-2025): 2024 WT and 3WT". Its 24-module page lists "Silverado EV 2024: RST First Edition and WT 4WT". So 3WT = Extended and 4WT = Max. The batch's inference from NRCan range (393 / 450 mi) is correct, and the Weekly Driver page should be cited for it.
- **Onboard charger.**
  - The 2026 PDF says: "11.5 kW high-voltage on 4WT. 19.2 kW module on 5WT and 8WT." The batch gives 11.5 kW for 2024-3wt and 2025-wt-extended-range, which contradicts this (Required changes 1).
  - The 2025 PDF lists "AC Offboarding Power (5WT, 8WT) 10.2 kW with 19.2 kW module". That also points to 19.2 kW on 5WT.

**Year GM moved to native NACS (2027): verified for all four models, US only.**
