## 2. Heat pump from ADAC (German market) and carry-overs
Opened ADAC pages (all say "Wärmepumpe: Serie" unless noted): i4 eDrive40 (11/21–06/24), i4 eDrive35 (11/22–06/24), i4 xDrive40 (from 07/24), i5 eDrive40 (from 10/23), i5 xDrive40 (from 03/24), i7 M70 (07/23–06/26), iX xDrive40 Edition Sport (03/24–01/25), Countryman SE Essential ALL4 (03/24–01/26), i3 94 Ah (07/16–08/17: option, 660 EUR). All quoted values are on the pages.

Is ADAC acceptable? For a feature that doesn't vary by market B94 allows it, but a heat pump does vary by market for BMW (the i3 below, MINI) and the brief requires a source saying the system is unchanged for any carry-over. Result per group:
- **i4 (2022–2026), standard: OK.** The BMW US 2022 release (src-w2-bmw-i4, opened) says "standard integrated heating and cooling system ... heat pump function". ADAC covers 11/21–06/24 and the facelift from 07/24, so the years are continuous. Keep, but the note "no change found 2023-2025" isn't a source: reword to name the ADAC periods. Note both the US 2022 release and ADAC: the US release names eDrive40 and M50 only; eDrive35 and M60 trims rest on ADAC eDrive35 (Germany). Acceptable with LC (already LC).
- **i7 (2023–2027), standard: OK for 2023–2026.** BMW US release (opened): "comes with a model-specific climate control unit featuring extremely efficient heat pump technology". Page doesn't use the word "standard" but it is the i7's own unit. ADAC M70 (07/23–06/26) agrees. **2027 i7**: the 2027 car is a refresh (a search result calls it a "Neue Klasse brain transplant"); nothing opened says the climate system is unchanged. Keep with LC but name that gap in the notes, or drop 2027.
- **i5 (2024–2027), standard: weak.** BMW US 2024 5 Series release (opened) says "heat pump technology in the BMW i5" and "interior and battery heated simultaneously with the heat pump function" but never says standard; it is in no equipment list or package. ADAC (Germany) says Serie for eDrive40 (from 10/23) and xDrive40 (from 03/24). Nothing covers M60 (batch relies on the US release only) or 2025–2027 ("2024-2027 compared" is asserted, no source says unchanged). InsideEVs 2027 has no heat pump mention. Keep 2024 eDrive40/xDrive40; the rest need LC and a note naming exactly which pages were read, or drop 2026–27.
- **iX 2022–2025 (13 cars), standard: carried back from a 2026 US release + a German xDrive40 page (03/24–01/25).** BMW US 2026 iX release is verified: "All BMW iX models are equipped with an advanced heat pump function as standard." It says nothing about earlier years. **Better source found (not in the batch):** BMW US 2022 iX xDrive50 press kit https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/usa/article/detail/T0334227EN_US/the-new-2022-bmw-ix-xdrive50 lists "Integrated Heating and Cooling System with Heat Pump Function" under the xDrive50 (found in a search; I did not open it). Use it for 2022–2025, replacing the ADAC xDrive40 carry. The 2022 and 2023 xDrive50/M60 rows currently cite ADAC xDrive40 (a trim and years the car didn't have).
- **Countryman SE 2025–2027:** ADAC verified for 03/24–01/26 Germany only. NA availability unconfirmed (batch says so). 2027 is beyond the ADAC period (ends 01/26). No source that the US car has it. Mark `estimated`/LC or drop 2027.
- **iX3 2027 50 xDrive (2 cars):** EVMagz (opened) says "BMW equips the iX3 40 with a heat pump as standard"; it says nothing about the 50 xDrive explicitly (only "no major differences in standard equipment levels") and is European. The batch labels it kind community, LC: it is an inference from the iX3 40 to the 50 xDrive, so it should be `estimated` with both named. Electrive 2026-05-29 reports the same for the iX3 40. A search result (Onyx Automotive dealer blog) says standard on the US iX3; I did not open it. A BMW US page for the 2027 iX3 50 xDrive (newsroom) was not found to mention a heat pump.
- **Original i3 (`optional`, 2014–2020, 9 cars/trims): REJECT carry.** ADAC page (opened) shows heat pump optional, 660 EUR, for the 94 Ah, 07/16–08/17, Germany. The batch applies it to 60 Ah (2014–2016), 120 Ah (2019–20) and the i3s with the note "no page read for this battery/year" and calls it `manufacturer`. A search turned up non-official forum/parts-site statements that the **US** i3 had the heat pump as standard before MY2019 and optional from 2019 (REx never), the opposite of the German list; so the German option is not safe for North America. Not verified with an official page. Action: drop all i3 heat pumps unless a US or Canadian BMW source (i3 press kit, window-sticker option S4T9A) is found.
- Source `type` for the ADAC source records in the batch is `community`; the repo's existing ADAC sources are `testing_org`. Use the same type.
- Kind: heat pump from ADAC/spec lists is relayed manufacturer equipment, so `manufacturer` is acceptable for the verified pages (rule 4); not for the carried years.
