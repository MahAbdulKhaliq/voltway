## Specific answers to the risk list

1. **2026-27 `_sold` estimates.** Not allowed. No openable source says sold or in box for 2026+. Volvo's page itself is MY2025-only; the Volvo Canada support page lists "EX90, EX30, EX40, EC40, XC40 Recharge and C40 Recharge" as able to access the network and says purchase at a retailer, but that is only a search snippet (403). Dealer blog (volvocarslisle.com, opened): "Starting with model year 2025, the Volvo EX90 and other select models will include a NACS adapter as standard equipment." The EX30 is not named. I found no openable source for the 2026 EX30 in the box. Options: (a) cite the Volvo page + Canadian-support snippet as unverifiable and leave 2026-27 blank; (b) keep the value as `low_confidence` with kind `manufacturer` only if the owner accepts that Volvo's "MY2025 and later" wording is what the (blocked) support page says. The checker cannot verify that wording.
2. **EX60.** Reject the estimate. A 2023 article about "future Volvos" doesn't name the EX60 and the stated adapter direction is the opposite one. The NACS port also needs a cited source. Drop `adapters_included` for both EX60 cars, or find an EX60 press page.
3. **Polestar 2 heat pump.** `optional` by year is supported for 2021 (Plus pack), 2023, 2024 dual, 2025 CA. Weak points: 2024 single motor (page silent), 2025 US performance-pack (Carvana says standard), and Launch Edition 2021 `none` (inference from silence).
4. **Polestar 3/4 and Lotus.** Spec pages show the heat pump without a pack qualifier but without a model year, so low_confidence is right. Lotus Canada page is better than Carwow.
5. **Unfilled Volvo heat pumps.** I could not find an openable North American source. The Volvo Canada EX30 specs page (403) shows up in search as listing a heat pump; the EX40/EC40/EX90 pages did not surface. Automotive Fleet's 2025 lineup article (opened) has no heat pump text. Try: Car and Driver, MotorTrend, InsideEVs, Guide Auto or Driving.ca spec pages per model; Wikipedia pages for EX30, EX40/XC40 Recharge, EC40/C40 Recharge, EX90; Auto-Data (allowed, B109).
