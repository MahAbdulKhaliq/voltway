## Fix list (all minor)

1. **Cayenne S Electric 2026 and Taycan 4 2025:** notes say "(CA/US)" but the cited pages are US newsrooms. Say "(US)" in the notes. The Taycan GTS has a CA page, so "(CA/US)" holds there. Wheels can differ by market, so the label should match the source.
2. **BMW i5 M60 2024, 20 and 21 inch options:** the page names them for the 5 Series generally, not for the M60. Say so in the notes. `low_confidence` is already set, so only the wording changes.
3. **Macan 2024 variants:** the 21-inch size is cited to the MY2025 release for a 2024 variant. Add "MY2025 release, applied to 2024" to the notes. The 22-inch "optional" reading is the researcher's inference from "up-to-22-inch".

Spanning-trim rule: each of these variants has one trim, and the notes name it. No spanning problems found. Every variant with wheels has a standard size.
